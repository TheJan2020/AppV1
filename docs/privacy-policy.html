Privacy Policy

Primewave AI – Smart Home Control (Android)

by PRIMEWAVE AI SOLUTIONS

Effective date: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Primewave AI (“the app”) is a smart home control application published by PRIMEWAVE AI SOLUTIONS (“we”, “our”, “us”). The app connects to your own Home Assistant server and, optionally, to AI voice and chat services so you can control your home by touch, voice or text. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

2. Information the app handles

Server link and sign-in detailsThe server link you enter, the user you select and the password you type. They are sent only to your own Home Assistant server so you can sign in. The server link and the access token you receive after signing in are stored on your device in Android’s encrypted storage (Android Keystore).
Push notification tokenA device token used to deliver smart home alerts. It is sent to your own backend server and routed through Expo’s push notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to reach your device.
Voice audioCaptured only while you actively use the Butler voice assistant. The audio is sent to the AI backend you configure (and to the AI provider that backend uses, such as Google Gemini or OpenAI) to be turned into text and to answer your request. We do not store it permanently.
Chat messagesText you type to the Butler assistant is sent to the same AI backend and AI provider to process your request.
Device nameYour Android device model name, sent once to your own backend when you register for notifications, so you can manage your devices.
PreferencesSettings such as your preferred AI model and room layout, stored locally on your device.

Primewave AI does not create or store user accounts. You sign in with a user account that already exists on your own Home Assistant server, and that server is managed by you or by the person who set it up for you.

PRIMEWAVE AI SOLUTIONS does not operate a cloud database or analytics service for this app, and we do not receive your credentials, audio, messages or smart home data on servers that we run. The app does not contain advertising and does not use the Android advertising ID.

3. How the information is used

  • To connect to your Home Assistant server and display and control your devices.
  • To send your voice or text requests to the Butler assistant and carry out your commands.
  • To deliver push notifications about events in your home that you set up on your own backend.
  • To remember your preferences so the app works as you configured it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Permissions

MicrophoneUsed only for the Butler voice assistant, and only while you use the voice feature. Voice features are optional.
NotificationsUsed to show smart home alerts sent from your own backend. Notifications are optional and can be turned off at any time.
InternetRequired to reach your Home Assistant server, your backend and the AI services you choose to use.

You can change or revoke permissions at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Primewave AI → Permissions.

5. Third-party services

The app works with the following services, which you choose to use and configure. Their own privacy policies apply to data they receive.

  • Home Assistant – your own smart home server. Data is exchanged between the app and that server.
  • Google Gemini – if your Butler backend uses Gemini, your voice or text requests are processed by Google’s API (Google Privacy Policy).
  • OpenAI (optional) – if you enter an OpenAI API key, voice transcription is sent to OpenAI (OpenAI Privacy Policy).
  • Expo Push Notifications and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging – used to deliver notifications to your device (Expo Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy).

Primewave AI is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Home Assistant.

6. Security

Credentials are stored using Android’s encrypted storage. We recommend that your Home Assistant server and backend are reached over HTTPS so that data is encrypted in transit. If you connect using a plain http:// address, data sent to that server is not encrypted in transit. How data is protected on your own servers depends on how you set them up.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored credentials and preferences from your device. You can also go to Android Settings → Apps → Primewave AI → Storage → Clear data.
  • Logging out of the app sends a request to your backend to delete your push token. User accounts themselves are managed on your Home Assistant server, so removing a user account is done there.
  • Data held on your own Home Assistant server or backend, or by an AI provider you use, is managed by you or by that provider under its own policy.

8. Your choices

  • Do not enter AI keys or configure a Butler backend if you do not want to use AI features. They are optional.
  • Do not grant microphone or notification permission if you do not want those features.
  • Contact us to ask questions about your information or to request help with deletion.

9. Children

Primewave AI is intended for adults who manage a home. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
