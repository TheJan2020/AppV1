Privacy Policy

Primewave — Smart Home Control

by PRIMEWAVE AI SOLUTIONS

Effective Date: June 15, 2026

👋 Introduction

Welcome to Primewave, a product of PRIMEWAVE AI SOLUTIONS ("we", "our", or "us"). Primewave is a smart home control application that connects to your personal Home Assistant instance and optional AI voice/chat services. Your privacy is important to us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your rights. By using Primewave you agree to the practices described below.

📋 Information We Collect

Primewave accesses the following information solely to provide its features:

  • Home Assistant credentials — your Home Assistant server URL and long-lived access token, stored securely on your device using iOS Secure Enclave (SecureStore).
  • Device push token — an Expo push notification token registered with your own backend server so your server can send you smart home alerts.
  • Voice audio — microphone audio captured only while you are actively using the voice assistant (Butler). Audio is sent to your own backend for transcription/AI processing and is not stored permanently.
  • User preferences — app settings (e.g., preferred AI model, room layout preferences) stored locally on your device.
  • Device name — your iOS device model name, sent once to your own backend when registering for push notifications, solely for your own device management.
Important: Primewave does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any Primewave-operated servers. All data goes directly between your device and your own self-hosted Home Assistant instance and/or backend.

⚙️ How We Use Information

  • Connecting to your Home Assistant instance to display and control smart home devices.
  • Sending voice/text messages to your own AI backend (Butler) to process commands and answer questions about your home.
  • Delivering push notifications from your own backend about smart home events (door opened, motion detected, etc.).
  • Remembering your preferences locally so the app works the way you configured it.
  • Authenticating you via Face ID / Touch ID (biometrics are handled entirely by iOS — Primewave never sees or stores biometric data).

🔐 Device Permissions

Primewave requests the following iOS permissions:

Permission Purpose
Microphone Required for the Butler voice assistant. Audio is only recorded when you actively hold the microphone button. It is sent to your own backend for processing and is never stored permanently.
Local Network Required to connect to your Home Assistant server and Butler backend running on your local network.
Push Notifications Used to receive smart home alerts (e.g., door opened, security triggered) sent from your own backend server.
Face ID / Biometrics Optional — used to protect access to the app. All biometric authentication is handled by iOS; Primewave never receives or stores biometric data.
Camera (CCTV view) Primewave displays live camera streams from your Frigate/Home Assistant cameras. No camera data is stored or transmitted outside your own network.

🔒 Data Storage & Security

All sensitive credentials (Home Assistant URL, access token, API keys) are stored exclusively on your device using iOS Secure Enclave (SecureStore). They are never transmitted to any third-party server operated by us.

Your push notification token is registered with your own backend server. We have no access to your backend, your token, or the notifications it sends.

Primewave does not operate any cloud database or analytics service. There is no Primewave server that stores your data.

🔗 Third-Party Services

Primewave may interact with the following third-party services at your direction and using credentials you provide:

  • Home Assistant — your self-hosted smart home platform. Data is exchanged over your own network.
  • OpenAI (optional) — if you choose to enter an OpenAI API key in Settings, voice transcription (Whisper) is sent directly to OpenAI's API. This is governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini (optional) — if your Butler backend is configured to use Gemini, queries are processed by Google's API under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo Push Notifications — push tokens are routed through Expo's notification gateway to reach APNs (Apple Push Notification service). No message content is stored by Expo beyond delivery. See Expo's Privacy Policy.
  • Frigate (optional) — a self-hosted NVR. Camera data stays entirely within your local network.

👶 Children's Privacy

Primewave is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Primewave is a professional tool intended for home owners and smart home enthusiasts.

🗓️ Data Retention

Since Primewave stores all data locally on your device or on your own servers, you have full control over retention:

  • Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all locally stored credentials and preferences immediately.
  • To remove your push token from your backend, log out of the app — this automatically sends a delete request to your backend's notification endpoint.

⚖️ Your Rights

Because Primewave does not collect or store your data on any Primewave-operated servers, most privacy rights (access, correction, deletion) are exercised directly on your own device and infrastructure. Specifically:

  • Access & Deletion — delete the app to remove all local data, or clear data from your own backend.
  • Revoke permissions — you can revoke microphone, notification, or biometric permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Primewave.
  • Opt out of AI features — do not enter API keys in Settings; all AI features are strictly opt-in.

📝 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
